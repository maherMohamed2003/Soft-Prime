ABOUT SOFT PRIME

OUR STORY

Started with one question: why is this still on paper?

Soft Prime grew out of building real systems for real small businesses — a gym tracking members on spreadsheets, a store counting stock by hand, a delivery team losing orders in WhatsApp chats. Every project taught us something new about how businesses actually run, and we turned that into a practice: custom digital systems and websites for shops, clinics, gyms, retailers and service companies.

Today we design and build end-to-end — structure, interface, and logic — so every client gets a system shaped around their business, not the other way around.
