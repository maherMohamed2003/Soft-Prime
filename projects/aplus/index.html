ENGINEERING & MULTI-BRANCH OPS

A Plus — Multi-Branch Licensing & Finishing Platform

← All Projects

A Plus is a multi-branch operations platform for a licensing-and-finishing company — every branch gets its own dashboard, clients, payroll and expenses, while jobs move between two departments (Rokhas/licensing and Tashteeb/finishing) with live profit tracking and one-click offline Excel exports.

KEY FEATURES

What the system does

Multi-Branch Switching

Every branch runs its own dashboard, clients, payroll and expenses under one account.

Dual Department Dashboards

Separate Rokhas (licensing) and Tashteeb (finishing) dashboards, each with its own live totals.

Per-Job Financial Tracking

Price, payments, expenses, profit and balance tracked automatically for every job.

Client & Job History

A searchable client directory showing every client's job count and contact details.

Payroll & Office Expenses

Per-branch salary records and office expense logs, filterable by date.

Offline Excel Reports

Generate and download job, revenue and expense reports as Excel files with no internet connection.
