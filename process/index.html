HOW WE WORK

From Idea to Live System

Five clear stages, the same for every project — no surprises, no guessing where things stand.

01
STEP 01 · SYS.DISCOVERY

Discovery Call

We talk through how your business actually runs today — on paper, in spreadsheets, or scattered across WhatsApp — and where it's costing you time or money.

02
STEP 02 · SYS.PROPOSAL

Proposal & Scope

You get a clear, itemized proposal — what's included, what it costs and how long it takes — generated through our own proposal system, the same one shown in our projects.

03
STEP 03 · SYS.DESIGN

Design & Structure

We map out the database structure and design every screen — in Arabic RTL or English, whichever your team actually uses day to day.

04
STEP 04 · SYS.BUILD

Development & Testing

The system gets built and tested against real scenarios from your business — not just sample data — so it works correctly from day one.

05
STEP 05 · SYS.LAUNCH

Delivery & Support

You get the finished, production-ready system with a walkthrough for your team — and ongoing support as your business grows and needs change.
